How to play

  1. The deck is shuffled and split equally between you and the computer.
  2. You always see your top card; the computer’s card stays face down until the round resolves.
  3. Click a category on your card to compare that stat.
  4. The higher value wins both cards — except on a stat marked ↓, where the lowest value wins. Won cards go to the bottom of the winner’s pile.
  5. On a draw the cards go to a pot that the next round’s winner claims.
  6. If the computer wins a round, it picks the next category.
  7. The game ends when one player holds every card.

You win!
